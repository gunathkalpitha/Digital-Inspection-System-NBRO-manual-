1. Introduction

The NBRO app supports pre-crack building condition surveys, disaster-risk assessment and structural-defect capture across Sri Lanka.

  • Works offline. Every survey saves on the device first and uploads automatically when internet returns.
  • Four user roles. Each person sees only the data and tools their role allows.
  • Invitation-only accounts. Users activate through an official email link; direct password creation is disabled.
  • Privacy built into the database. Access rules are enforced in the cloud database, not only on screen.
  • Official reports. One tap produces a multi-page PDF using SER & PMD defect notation.

2. System overview & features

The NBRO Site Inspection System brings site registration, building observations and defect records into one system. A web application serves Administrators; a mobile application serves Field Officers on site.

Web application (Administrators)

  • Secure login based on role
  • Officer account management
  • Access to all site and defect records
  • Notice system with priority levels and read tracking
  • Profile and photo management
  • Send notices to all or selected officers

Mobile application (Field Officers)

  • Login and profile management
  • Site registration: owner, building reference, address, GPS location
  • General observations, external services (water, sewage, electricity), main building details and ancillary structures
  • Defects: notation, category, floor level, location, measurements, remarks, photographs
  • Building front-view photos
  • Sync to server; read administrator notices

Architecture

Typical workflow

  1. Admin invites officer
  2. Officer activates account
  3. Survey on site
  4. Saved offline
  5. Auto-sync to cloud

4. Getting started & logging in

Accept your invitation

  1. Open the invitation email NBRO sent to your Gmail address.
  2. Tap the link and set your own password.
  3. Install and open the NBRO app on your Android device.

Log in

  1. Open the NBRO app.
  2. Enter your account email and password.
  3. Tap Log In. A welcome notification shows your name and role.

After your first password login you may be offered Use Biometric Login, depending on your device. If an administrator disables your account, you cannot sign in until it is re-enabled.

Account security. Never share passwords or invitation links, or put them in screenshots or documents.

5. Administrator guide

Covers Super Admin, Main Admin and Regional Admin. Tools you see depend on your tier.

Invite a user

  1. Open Account Management. Users appear in Active (count) and Disabled (count) tabs.
  2. Tap + Add User or + Add Officer.
  3. Enter the full name and Gmail address.
  4. Tap Send Invitation. The person receives a link and the account is linked to you as creator.
Important. “Create Account Directly” is disabled. Every account is activated through the invitation link.

Enable or disable a user

  1. Find the user card in the Active or Disabled tab.
  2. Tap Disable to suspend access; the card moves to Disabled.
  3. Tap Enable to restore access; the card moves to Active.

Super Admin and Main Admin accounts show a gold PROTECTED badge and cannot be disabled.

Review surveys and the map (Main and Regional Admins)

  1. Open Inspections (View All) to search, filter and review records.
  2. Open Sites on Map to see GPS markers.
  3. Select a marker to view the site record and photographs, or tap Export PDF.

6. Field Surveyor guide

Surveyors conduct inspections, capture defects, manage drafts and generate reports. They see only surveys they created.

  • Hero banner: welcome name, date and time.
  • Announcement bar: latest unread bulletins.
  • Notification bell: red badge for unread notices.
  • Saved Drafts: incomplete surveys to resume.
  • Service cards: New Inspection, Inspections, Map, Analytics, Reports.

Tap New Inspection on the dashboard to start the wizard below.

8. Drafts & existing inspections

Save and resume a draft

  1. Tap Save as Draft at the bottom of any wizard step.
  2. Open Saved Drafts on the dashboard.
  3. Tap Tap to resume.

Edit an inspection

  1. Open Inspections Portal or Inspections (View All).
  2. Select the record and tap the Edit icon.
  3. Change details, defects or photos, then tap Save Changes.

9. Announcements & notifications

Publish a notice (administrators)

  1. Open Notices and tap Create Notice.
  2. Enter a title and message, then choose recipients.
  3. Select Urgent, High or Normal priority.
  4. Tap Send Announcement.

Read notices

  1. A red badge on the bell means unread notices.
  2. Tap the bell or Notices; unread items show UNREAD.
  3. Tap a notice to mark it read, or use Mark All Read.

Bulletins also scroll in the dashboard ticker.

10. Official PDF survey reports

11. Offline working & sync

  • Surveys, drafts and photos are stored on the device and work with no connection.
  • A background service uploads pending records when internet is detected.
  • Do not clear app data until all surveys have synchronised.
  • After sync you see only the records your role permits.

12. Data security & isolation

Row Level Security in the cloud database applies these rules even if the app screens are bypassed.

RuleEffect
Own dataEvery user can access records they created.
Main AdminAccess to all surveys and map pins nationwide.
Regional AdminAccess to records of users they created (their branch).
Super AdminNo access to citizen survey data, photos or GPS.
Disabled usersCannot sign in until re-enabled.

Technical reference (IT staff)

  SELECT target_user_id = auth.uid()
      OR public.is_main_admin()
      OR EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM public.profile p
                 WHERE p.id = target_user_id
                   AND p.created_by = auth.uid());
$$;

17. Frequently asked questions

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes. Surveys and photos save on your device first and upload automatically when a connection returns. Do not clear app data until everything has synced.

Why can't I create an account directly?

For security, every account is created by email invitation. The invited person sets their own password through the official link.

Why can't I see another branch's surveys?

Access is limited by role and enforced in the database. Regional Admins see only officers they created; Field Surveyors see only their own surveys.

How do I stop someone using the app?

Open Account Management and tap Disable on their card. Tap Enable to restore access. Super Admin and Main Admin accounts are protected and cannot be disabled.

Where is my PDF report saved?

In your device's Download folder, named NBRO_Inspection_[RefNo]_[Timestamp].pdf. You can open or share it straight after export.

What if a survey was saved halfway?

Use Save as Draft on any step, then resume from Saved Drafts on the dashboard. Final saves are atomic: a survey is stored completely or not at all.
